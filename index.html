The PTx clinical edge

GP-practice diagnosis skills, with time to go the extra mile

A First Contact Practitioner (FCP) is the physiotherapist many GP surgeries now offer as the first point of contact for muscle, bone and joint problems. At PTx you get that front-line judgement, with the time NHS appointments rarely allow.

Diagnostic precision

FCP work is about accurate diagnosis and helping people manage their own problem. That’s the standard I bring to every assessment.

  • Assessing complex or unclear problems at the front line
  • Knowing when it’s a physio problem, and when it needs a scan, a GP or a specialist
  • No GP referral needed to see me privately

Unhurried care

Private practice lets me combine accurate diagnosis with hands-on treatment and a tailored, long-term self-management plan.
