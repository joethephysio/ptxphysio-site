Your first visit

What to expect from your first 60 minutes

Nervous about a first appointment? Most people are. Here is exactly how it goes.

We listen

You tell me your story: what hurts, when it started, what you’ve tried and what you want to get back to. There is no rush, and nothing is too small.

We assess

A movement and hands-on assessment to work out what’s driving the problem, with an honest explanation in plain English. If you need a scan or a different specialist, I’ll say so.

You get a plan

A clear, tailored plan including treatment and exercises you can do yourself. Any further sessions are agreed together, with no pressure to rebook.

What to wear

Something comfortable you can move in, such as shorts or leggings and a T-shirt. You only need to expose the area being assessed, and you’re in control throughout.

What to bring

  • Any scan reports or letters about the problem
  • A list of any medication you take
  • Trainers or kit if the problem is with running or sport

You’ll be seen by an HCPC-registered, CSP member physiotherapist with 11+ years’ experience, including as a First Contact Practitioner.
