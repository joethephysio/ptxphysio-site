What we treat

Muscle, bone and joint problems of all kinds

If it affects how you move, I can help you understand it. If it needs a scan or a different specialist, I’ll tell you.

Joint & Muscle Pain

Pain in a joint or the muscles around it, often after a twist, a strain or a spell of doing more than usual.

Common examples

  • Shoulder, hip and knee pain
  • Ankle sprains
  • Muscle strains and tightness

How PTx helps

  • A thorough assessment to find where the pain is really coming from
  • Hands-on treatment where it helps, and exercises to rebuild strength
  • Clear advice on what to keep doing and what to ease off

Sports & Running Injuries

Injuries from training, running, cycling, rugby and gym work, and the question everyone asks: when can I get back to it?

Common examples

  • Running and cycling niggles and overuse
  • Hamstring and calf strains
  • Rugby, football and gym injuries

How PTx helps

  • Looking at how your training has changed, not just the sore spot
  • A progressive plan to return to your sport safely
  • Honest guidance on what you can do while you recover

Tendon & Overuse Issues

Pain that builds with repetition and is often worse at the start of activity.

Common examples

  • Achilles and heel pain
  • Tennis and golfer’s elbow
  • Knee, hip and shoulder tendon pain

How PTx helps

  • Education about why tendons get sore and how they recover
  • Gradual loading programmes, which take patience but usually work
  • Adjusting activity so you can keep moving

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Structured rehab to rebuild strength, movement and confidence after an operation.

Common examples

  • After knee, hip or shoulder surgery
  • After ligament or tendon repair
  • Getting back to normal activity

How PTx helps

  • Working alongside your surgeon’s guidance and any protocol you’ve been given
  • A phased plan that builds up as you heal
  • Support to regain confidence in the joint

Neck, Back & Posture Pain

Stiff necks and aching backs are common, and rarely as damaged as they feel.

Common examples

  • Lower back pain and sciatica
  • Stiff or painful necks
  • Desk-related aches

How PTx helps

  • An assessment that rules out anything more serious
  • Reassurance and education: your spine is built to move and load
  • Exercise-led care and hands-on treatment where it helps

Long-Term Condition Management

Ongoing muscle and joint problems that come and go, and need a plan you can live with.

Common examples

  • Persistent joint pain, including arthritis-related pain
  • Recurring flare-ups
  • Pain that has lasted months or years

How PTx helps

  • Practical tools to manage pain day to day
  • Strengthening and activity plans that fit your life
  • Support when things flare, for as long as you need it

When to get urgent help: call 999 or go to A&E for chest pain, a serious injury or if you cannot put weight through a leg after a fall. Call NHS 111 for numbness between your legs, new trouble controlling your bladder or bowels, or a hot, swollen joint with a fever. This website is not for emergencies.
