Privacy notice

Who I am: PTx Physiotherapy (Joe, sole practitioner), joe@ptxphysio.co.uk.

What I collect: the details you enter when you book.

Why: to arrange and deliver your appointment and, if you become a patient, to keep your clinical record. Health information is special category data, which I use with your explicit consent and to provide healthcare.

Who sees it: only me. Online bookings and clinical records are held in Cliniko, the practice-management software I use, which processes them on my behalf. I don’t sell or share your details, except where the law requires it or to protect your safety.

How long: clinical records are kept securely for as long as professional guidance requires. Enquiries that don’t lead to an appointment are deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Prefer to book another way? Email joe@ptxphysio.co.uk. PTx is not currently registered with any private medical insurers, so appointments are self-pay. If you plan to claim through your insurer, check with them before booking.
