When & where

Evenings and weekends

Most clinics run 9–5, Monday to Friday. PTx is built around your week instead, and Thursday clinics are also available. Appointments are held in a private clinic room in the Swindon and Malmesbury area, with the location confirmed when you book.

Home or gym visits may be possible at an extra cost. Get in touch to discuss.

Payment & insurance

Self-pay, with no referral needed

PTx is not currently registered with any private medical insurers, so appointments are self-pay. If your policy lets you claim for physiotherapy outside its approved network, ask your insurer before you book. I can provide a receipt for your records. You don’t need a GP referral to see me.
